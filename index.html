Questions we hear most

Can you guarantee my visa?+

No. Only the embassy decides. We make sure your file is honest, complete and consistent, and we tell you before you pay if we think it is not ready.

Why do you check my bank statements yourselves?+

Officers look for unexplained deposits and statements that do not match your income. We get statements from your bank directly and ask you to explain large credits before anything is submitted.

What if you decline my application?+

You pay nothing for the service. We explain why in writing and suggest a stronger route, such as a different destination or a plan to build your profile.

Is my passport safe with you?+

Every handover is signed for, passports travel in sealed envelopes by insured courier, and your documents are stored in company systems only.
